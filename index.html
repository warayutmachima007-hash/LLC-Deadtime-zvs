The ZVS window

Dead time must be longer than the node swing and shorter than the moment the tank current reverses.

How to choose the dead time

tdead≥2·Co(tr)·VinILm,pk

Long enough for the magnetizing current to swing the switch node [1], but short enough that the gate turns on before the tank current reverses. Use the highest switching frequency, where the magnetizing current is smallest, and confirm on the scope that Vds reaches 0 V just before the gate turns on.
